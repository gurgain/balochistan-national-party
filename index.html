About Us

Rooted in the people, guided by principle

The Balochistan National Party (BNP) is a democratic political party founded in 1996 by Sardar Ataullah Mengal. Headquartered in Quetta, the party works to represent the people of Balochistan in provincial and national politics.

We believe in constitutional rights, provincial autonomy, and the right of the people of Balochistan to decide how their land and resources are used. Our work is grounded in the rich language, culture and traditions of the region.

Our Vision

What we stand for

Rights & Dignity

Protection of fundamental human rights and the rule of law for every citizen of Balochistan.

Provincial Autonomy

Real self-governance, with decisions about Balochistan made by the people of Balochistan.

Ownership of Resources

Fair control over and benefit from the province's natural wealth, coastline and minerals.

Language & Culture

Preserving and promoting the Balochi and Brahui languages, heritage and traditions.

Our Journey

Milestones

  1. 1996

    The Balochistan National Party is founded under the leadership of Sardar Ataullah Mengal.

  2. 1997

    Sardar Akhtar Mengal becomes Chief Minister of Balochistan.

  3. 2012

    The party presents its Six Points for the rights of Balochistan before the Supreme Court.

  4. Today

    Led by Sardar Akhtar Mengal, the party continues its democratic struggle for the province.
